What we manage

Ecommerce email management from strategy to reporting.

Build a consistent programme for subscribers, first-time buyers and returning customers, using Omnisend.

Account audit and strategy

Review existing flows, list sources, sender configuration and reporting. Agree priorities around your store's traffic, buying cycle and available content.

Lifecycle automation

Plan and build automated email flows for welcome, unfinished purchases, post-purchase follow-up and lapsed customers, with eligibility and exit rules.

Campaigns and segmentation

Create an agreed campaign calendar, copy and email designs. Use engagement and purchase behaviour to choose relevant audiences.

Measurement and improvement

Review clicks, orders, unsubscribes and attributed revenue alongside costs and store performance. Use the email measurement guide to interpret the reports.

How the engagement works

  1. Audit: review account access, existing journeys, integrations and the main gaps.
  2. Plan: agree flow priorities, campaign frequency, content responsibilities and measurement.
  3. Build and test: check templates, links, customer events, exclusions and purchase exits before launch.
  4. Manage: run the agreed calendar and review performance to decide what to improve next.

Which service fits your store?

Choose Omnisend management for platform-specific setup and support, or Shopify email marketing for journeys built around Shopify customer events. Our case studies describe individual account results rather than forecasts for your store.

Cost, scope and launch timing

Core implementation can follow a seven-day plan once access, content and approvals are ready; launch timing does not guarantee a revenue outcome.

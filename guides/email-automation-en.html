BUILD THE FOUNDATION FIRST

Flows and campaigns do different jobs.

A flow responds to a customer event or condition. A campaign is a planned message to a selected audience at a chosen time. Both need valid audience permissions, relevant content and frequency control.

CompareAutomated flowCampaign
Starts withA customer eventA scheduled send
ExampleCheckout without an orderA new collection launch
ReviewEligibility, filters and exitsAudience, offer and timing

Prioritise the useful basics

Check order and delivery events and list eligibility first. Establish welcome and checkout recovery, then add journeys as traffic justifies them. For an older programme, audit links, stock, offers and exit rules before extending sequences. More messages are not automatically more useful.

SET THE FOUNDATION

Before you build

Check store events, currency and product links. Send marketing only to eligible contacts and respect unsubscribes and suppressions. Keep essential transactional updates separate from promotions, and agree who approves copy and design.

Make the promised first impression, explain the product and help a new subscriber make an informed first purchase.

Messages in this example

Filters & re-entry

Exit rules

Site abandonment

Offer a useful route back to the catalogue when someone explored the site but did not find a specific product. Keep this broad, helpful and unobtrusive.

Trigger intent

An identified eligible contact is active on the site without viewing a product.

Messages in this example

1 email · test against your audience

Filters & re-entry

Exclude product views, basket additions, checkouts and orders since entry. Start by allowing no more than one entry in 30 days.

Exit rules

Exit when the customer views a product, adds to the basket, starts checkout or buys.

Flow map & message plan

What each email does

  1. After a test delay, for example 6 hours · Offer help and link to useful categories

Abandoned cart

Recover interest at the basket stage. This is different from checkout abandonment: the customer has not yet begun the payment process.

Trigger intent

A known eligible contact adds a product to the basket but does not start checkout.

Messages in this example

5 emails · test against your audience

Filters & re-entry

Wait for inactivity; an example starting point is one hour. Suppress overlapping native store reminders. Verify basket restoration and live stock.

Exit rules

Exit on checkout start or an order. Recheck eligibility before every email.

Flow map & message plan

What each email does

  1. 1 hour · Show the current basket
  2. Day 1 · Explain a relevant product benefit
  3. Day 2 · Share verified customer feedback
  4. Day 3 · Answer delivery or product questions
  5. Day 5 · Optional margin-safe offer, with real conditions

Message counts and delays are example test plans, not Omnisend requirements or guarantees. Start smaller if your audience or engagement does not justify a longer sequence.

Abandoned checkout

Help a high-intent shopper complete a purchase. Keep the final total, currency, delivery costs and payment information consistent with the store.

Trigger intent

A known eligible contact starts checkout but does not place an order.

Messages in this example

5 emails · test against your audience

Filters & re-entry

Wait for inactivity, test the recovery link, and check whether your ecommerce platform already sends the same reminder.

Exit rules

Exit immediately after an order. Do not run the cart sequence alongside checkout recovery.

Flow map & message plan

What each email does

  1. 1 hour · Restore the unfinished checkout
  2. 12 hours · Explain delivery and support options
  3. Day 1 · Address a common objection
  4. Day 3 · Optional offer if permissions and margins allow
  5. Day 5 · One final helpful reminder, without invented urgency

Message counts and delays are example test plans, not Omnisend requirements or guarantees. Start smaller if your audience or engagement does not justify a longer sequence.

Post-purchase experience

Thank the buyer, reduce uncertainty, explain how to use the product and invite honest feedback. A review request belongs naturally inside this journey.

Trigger intent

An order is placed; delivery-dependent messages wait for confirmed delivery.

Messages in this example

4 emails · test against your audience

Filters & re-entry

Separate essential receipts and shipping updates from marketing. Check product relevance, delivery status and unresolved support cases.

Exit rules

Stop or divert unsuitable messages after cancellation, refund or a support issue; avoid repeating the sequence during an overlapping order.

Flow map & message plan

What each email does

  1. After order · Acknowledge and thank, without duplicating the receipt
  2. After delivery · Send setup or care instructions
  3. After useful product experience · Ask for honest feedback
  4. At a relevant later point · Offer a complementary product to eligible customers

Message counts and delays are example test plans, not Omnisend requirements or guarantees. Start smaller if your audience or engagement does not justify a longer sequence.

Win-back and replenishment

Give previous buyers a relevant reason to return. Consumables may need replenishment reminders; other categories may need product updates or a preference check.

Trigger intent

The customer reaches a relevant repurchase interval or a genuine lapse, for example 60 days where the data supports it.

Messages in this example

3 emails · test against your audience

Filters & re-entry

Exclude recent purchasers and consistently inactive or ineligible contacts. Coordinate with regular campaigns rather than adding unnecessary messages.

Exit rules

Exit after a new purchase, an unsubscribe or a change in eligibility.

Flow map & message plan

What each email does

  1. At the selected lapse · Ask what is useful now
  2. 4 days later · Share a relevant update or recommendation
  3. 10 days later · Offer a preference choice; suppress if there is no useful response

Message counts and delays are example test plans, not Omnisend requirements or guarantees. Start smaller if your audience or engagement does not justify a longer sequence.

KEEP THE PROGRAMME USEFUL

A practical optimisation routine

Keep a simple change log: what changed, for whom, which metric matters and when you will review it. Avoid simultaneous overlapping tests whose results you cannot interpret.

Use campaigns to learn about content

Test useful content angles in suitable campaigns: product education, comparisons or delivery questions. Adapt a promising idea for a relevant flow, then validate it again. A campaign winner is not automatically a flow winner because audience, intent and timing differ.

Product education

Try a buying guide in a campaign, then adapt the idea for welcome.

Care and usage

Develop useful content for the period after confirmed delivery.

Reduce uncertainty

Test a clear delivery explanation in a recovery email.

Tests to run inside a flow

  • Delay: compare two timings while keeping content and audience conditions consistent.
  • Length: a concise message versus a fuller answer to one buying question.
  • Subject and preheader: clear wording versus a different truthful presentation.
  • Format: plain text versus a visual layout with the same core information.
  • Recommendations: a relevant category versus a specific product when the data supports it.

Choose the success metric before testing and consider clicks, orders and costs together. Do not declare a winner from a tiny sample or a small random fluctuation. Apply what the evidence supports and record the lesson.

Your pre-launch checklist

  • Place a test order and verify that recovery messages stop.
  • Check links, prices and discounts on mobile and in a real inbox.
  • Review campaign and flow overlap before increasing frequency.
  • For UAE audiences, verify AED prices, delivery coverage and local scheduling.
  • For bilingual emails, review Arabic wording, direction, numbers and buttons.
  • Record attribution settings and reporting windows before comparing results.
MEASURE WHAT MATTERS

Measure the full picture

Review clicks, orders and unsubscribes alongside costs, discounts and returns. Compare equal reporting windows and distinguish flows from campaigns. Revenue attributed to email is not proof that all those sales were additional purchases caused by it.

How the engagement works

  1. Audit: review account access, existing journeys, integrations and the main gaps.
  2. Plan: agree flow priorities, campaign frequency, content responsibilities and measurement.
  3. Build and test: check templates, links, customer events, exclusions and purchase exits before launch.
  4. Manage: run the agreed calendar and review performance to decide what to improve next.

Which service fits your store?

Choose Omnisend management for platform-specific setup and support, or Shopify email marketing for journeys built around Shopify customer events. Our case studies describe individual account results rather than forecasts for your store.

Cost, scope and launch timing

Management costs approximately AED 3,930 per month, excluding VAT. This includes the Omnisend subscription and four campaigns per month, with no setup fee and a three-month minimum term. Before starting, agree on the flow scope, the initial number of flows, the revision allowance, and any integration or migration work. Core implementation can follow a seven-day plan once access, content and approvals are ready; launch timing does not guarantee a revenue outcome.

The AED amount is an indicative conversion of our existing monthly fee using 1 GBP ≈ AED 4.91 on 9 November 2026. Exchange rates can change; your proposal confirms the final AED fee and billing terms. Exchange-rate reference.

Our managed service is designed for stores with monthly revenue of approximately AED 49,100 or more. Suitability also depends on your audience, traffic and buying cycle.

These are EmailFlow’s existing ecommerce case studies, brought together for UAE readers. They are not presented as UAE client results. The original reports retain their currencies, reporting windows and limitations so you can assess the evidence in context.

Building an email programme from the beginning

The from-zero case study describes the account’s reported email attribution and the work undertaken. For a UAE store starting out, use it to frame questions about the first customer journeys to build, account access and the events needed for reporting.

Before applying the approach, check your store’s actual traffic, customer permissions and buying cycle. The reported revenue is specific to that account and is not a target we promise to another store.

Fashion: review the buying journey

The fashion store case study provides an example of reported performance across two months. A UAE fashion brand can use the discussion to review product discovery, sizing questions, repeat purchase journeys and the customer information needed to segment messages.

Agree what your own store needs first: relevant product content, clear delivery information and any language-specific review. Comparing stores without accounting for list size, promotions and margins can give a misleading impression of the opportunity.

Supplements: compare reporting periods carefully

The supplements case study includes figures across different reporting periods. Read those windows before drawing a conclusion about growth. Consider whether replenishment reminders fit the actual product usage and purchase interval in your store.

Keep product statements and email content aligned with information your business has verified. A result from another account does not validate product claims or predict your store’s revenue.

Holiday campaigns: separate the offer from the channel

The holiday campaign case study describes reported attribution during promotional windows. For UAE campaign planning, the useful questions are how the calendar, stock, offers and exclusions fit together. The example is not evidence of a Ramadan or Eid result.

Use our Ramadan and Eid planning guide to adapt the operational checklist to your audience and fulfilment process.

How to use the results in your own decision

Keep the original GBP amounts when reading historical results rather than making them look like AED sales from a UAE client. Review attribution settings, reporting windows and costs alongside revenue. Attributed sales do not establish how many additional purchases email caused.

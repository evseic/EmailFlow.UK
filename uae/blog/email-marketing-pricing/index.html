Email Marketing Pricing in the UAE: Costs in AED

Published 9 November 2026EmailFlow

For a UAE ecommerce store, a useful email marketing budget separates the platform subscription, the work to build and manage emails, and any translation or integration work. Compare proposals against the same deliverables before comparing the monthly total.

What does EmailFlow cost in AED?

EmailFlow management is approximately AED 3,930 per month, excluding VAT, with the Omnisend subscription included, no setup fee and four campaigns per month. The minimum term is three months. The AED amount is an indicative conversion of the existing £800 monthly fee using approximately AED 4.91 per GBP on 9 November 2026. Your proposal confirms the final AED fee, billing currency and any other agreed charges; this is not a locked exchange rate.

View the dated exchange-rate reference. For the existing GBP terms, see our UK pricing guide.

Compare the work behind the price

Ask which customer journeys will be built first, how many campaigns are included and who supplies product information and approvals. An account with reliable tracking and approved templates needs different work from a store migrating customer data or rebuilding an existing programme.

  • Automation: confirm the initial number of flows, emails per flow, purchase exits and revision allowance.
  • Campaigns: confirm monthly volume, audience selection, copy, design and approval deadlines.
  • Software: confirm the included Omnisend scope and whether requested integrations add costs.
  • Reporting: ask for orders, clicks, unsubscribes and attributed revenue alongside the reporting window and attribution settings.

Budget for language and approvals

If your store serves English- and Arabic-speaking customers, define which emails need each language and how the correct version reaches the customer. Translation, Arabic copy review and right-to-left template checks need an agreed scope. They are not automatically included in the standard package.

Provide an approved glossary for product names, offers and delivery promises. Name the person who can approve each language. A campaign cannot be scheduled confidently if price, stock or translation approval is still outstanding.

Check whether the service fits your store

The core managed service is designed for stores with monthly revenue equivalent to £10,000 or more: approximately AED 49,100 at the dated rate above. Revenue alone does not establish suitability. List permissions, traffic, margins, purchase frequency and existing performance also affect the opportunity.

Use your own contribution margin to assess affordability. For example, if an agency fee is AED 3,930 and the contribution margin on additional sales is 40%, covering that fee would require AED 9,825 in additional sales before other costs. This is an illustrative calculation, not a forecast. Email-attributed sales are not necessarily additional sales caused by email.

Agree ownership and exit terms

Give one month’s notice to end management after the three-month minimum term. On exit, agency-created flows, templates, forms and designs are removed. You keep your account, subscriber data and reports. Confirm these terms and any additional work in writing before starting.

# EmailFlow expert video production pack

Production drafts, not published videos. Use a real practitioner, permissioned account recordings and accurate current platform interfaces. Do not show customer names, addresses or individual order data without permission. Titles do not promise a fixed revenue gain or AI citation.

## Video 1: How to audit a Omnisend cart recovery flow

Target: store owners deciding whether their existing automation is configured sensibly.

- 0:00 State the problem: a shopper should not receive a cart reminder after purchasing.
- 0:30 Show the actual integration and event used. Explain cart versus checkout entry in this account.
- 1:30 Inspect delays, eligibility and purchase checks before each message.
- 2:30 Demonstrate a test profile and personalisation fallbacks using synthetic test details.
- 3:30 Show links, mobile rendering and approval checks.
- 4:30 Explain how conversion attribution is configured and why attributed revenue is not proof of incremental growth.
- 5:30 Invite viewers to read https://www.emailflow.uk/omnisend-agency/ and the flow guide.

Description: A practical walkthrough of a Omnisend recovery flow by EmailFlow, London. We review entry events, purchase checks, timing and QA. Account settings vary; the example is not a revenue forecast. Website: https://www.emailflow.uk/omnisend-agency/ . Contact: hello@emailflow.uk .

## Video 2: Understanding an ecommerce email case study

Only record after the underlying figures, period, platform and sharing permission have been confirmed.

- Identify the reporting period, store context and exact starting situation.
- Show the permitted original export or screenshot, with personal data removed.
- Describe what changed and when: flows, campaign schedule and list sources.
- State attribution windows, currency, refunds and any other channels that changed.
- Distinguish reported email revenue from evidence of incremental store growth.
- Discuss limitations and what cannot be concluded from a before/after comparison.

Description links to the relevant case-study page rather than promising typical results.

## Publishing checklist

1. Record and edit the real demonstration; approve client material before publication.
2. Add an accurate title, custom readable thumbnail, captions and chapters.
3. Publish on the real EmailFlow channel. Add the relevant service-page link.
4. Create a watch page only when the video exists. Embed a playable video as the main content, add an edited transcript and stable thumbnail.
5. Add truthful VideoObject metadata (title, description, upload date, thumbnail and embed/content URL). Confirm current Google requirements at https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video .
6. Inspect video indexing and measure enquiries as well as views. No markup or script guarantees ranking or AI inclusion.

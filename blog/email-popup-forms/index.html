Email popup forms: timing, examples and Omnisend setup

Published on 2026-06-24 · Updated 30 October 2026 · EmailFlow

An email popup form is a signup form that appears over a store page and invites a visitor to subscribe. A useful form explains what the visitor will receive, lets them dismiss it easily and connects to a welcome email that delivers the promise. Start with the customer experience, then test timing and offers against your own store data.

In this guide: Display timing · Offer examples · Omnisend setup · Measurement

When should an email popup appear?

Give visitors room to understand the page before asking them to subscribe. A time delay, a scroll trigger or a visitor-opened teaser can each be worth testing. There is no single best delay for every store: a short product page and a long buying guide create different reading journeys.

Illustrative timing test: compare a 10-second delay with a 20-second delay while keeping the offer, audience and design the same. These are test inputs, not proven optimum settings. Review mobile and desktop separately, and avoid drawing a conclusion from a handful of signups.

Five ecommerce popup offer examples

The copy below is illustrative. Choose an offer you can fulfil and check its effect on margin before launching.

  1. First-order discount: “Join our emails for 10% off your first order.” Explain exclusions, expiry and minimum spend where relevant.
  2. Free delivery: “Get free UK delivery on your first order over £50.” Compare delivery cost with discount cost using our discount versus free-shipping example.
  3. Buying help: “Get our guide to choosing the right size.” Deliver something useful for the products on the page.
  4. Early access: “Hear about our next collection before launch.” Set an expectation you can maintain.
  5. Product education: “Get care tips for your new watch.” Match follow-up content to the subscriber’s interest instead of sending an unrelated promotion.

Omnisend signup-form setup checklist

Use the form editor’s behaviour settings to review triggers, audience, page targeting and reappearance frequency. Omnisend’s behaviour settings documentation explains the available controls. Multiple page triggers use OR logic: either condition can display the form. A custom trigger overrides the normal rules, so review it carefully if present.

  • Choose the form type and a single clear offer.
  • Decide who should see it and exclude pages where it interrupts buying. Check Omnisend’s targeting options for existing contacts and page rules.
  • Set a dismissal interval so closing the form does not immediately bring it back.
  • Check the email field, close control, keyboard focus and mobile layout on the actual store.
  • Submit with an authorised test address and verify the welcome email, promised asset or code and checkout restrictions.
  • Record the starting settings and the result of each check in the flow-audit worksheet.

Can email popups affect SEO?

Google recommends avoiding promotional overlays that prevent people from reaching the page content. A timer alone does not make an intrusive form safe. Use a modest footprint, an obvious close control and accessible content on mobile and desktop. See Google’s interstitial guidance for the current recommendations.

Measure signups and what happens afterwards

Form signup rate = completed signups ÷ form views × 100. For example, 30 signups from 1,000 form views is 3%. That calculation is an illustration, not an industry benchmark. Keep the denominator consistent when comparing versions; a visitor-opened form may reach a different audience from an automatic popup.

Alongside signup rate, review welcome-email delivery, meaningful clicks, orders, unsubscribes and contribution after the offer cost. More contacts do not automatically mean more profitable customers. Agree a test period and decision rule in advance; low traffic may leave the result inconclusive.

Frequently asked questions

Should a popup ask for a name and phone number?

Collect what the offer and follow-up need. Extra fields should have a clear purpose. Treat email and SMS preferences separately and explain what the subscriber is signing up to receive.

What is a good email popup conversion rate?

Use your store’s baseline for the same form type, traffic and device. A universal percentage hides differences in offers and audiences. Compare customer quality as well as form completion.

Why does my Omnisend form not appear?

Check whether it is published, its display rules, page and audience targeting, device visibility and dismissal frequency. Test an eligible visit without assuming a returning subscriber should see the acquisition offer.

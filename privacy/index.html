Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who is responsible for your information?

Erik Jevseicikas, operating as EmailFlow, is the controller for enquiries submitted through this website. Postal address: Naugarduko g. 15, LT-03226 Vilnius, Lithuania. Email info@emailflow.uk for privacy questions or to exercise your rights.

Information we collect

When you enquire, we collect your name, email address, store website, the phone number you optionally provide, and your answers about monthly revenue, email activity, list size and priorities. We record a submission timestamp. If you book a call, Calendly also processes your selected time and the details you provide in its booking journey.

Your name, email and website are required by our enquiry form so we can respond and understand the store. A phone number is optional. If you prefer, contact us by email instead of using the form.

How and why we use it

We use enquiry and booking details to respond, assess service suitability, arrange a consultation and discuss a possible engagement. Where you request steps towards a contract with us, that is the basis for the relevant processing. For enquiries made on behalf of a business and ordinary correspondence, our legitimate interest is responding to business enquiries and managing those relationships. We use necessary technical information to operate and protect the website.

The form uses a stated £10,000 monthly revenue threshold to guide suitability. Choosing “Under £10,000” shows guidance instead of the contact form. This is a service enquiry screen; you can contact us directly for a human discussion.

Services that receive information

  • Supabase: stores the enquiry record.
  • Telegram: receives an agency notification containing the supplied contact details and answers.
  • Calendly: provides scheduling. After an enquiry is accepted, your name and email are passed to its booking widget to prefill the booking fields. Submitting an enquiry alone does not book a call.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and provides site analytics.
  • Google Fonts: the site requests its web fonts from Google’s font services, which receive the technical information needed to deliver those requests.

See the providers’ own information: Supabase privacy notice, Telegram privacy policy, Calendly privacy notice, Vercel privacy notice and Google Fonts privacy information.

Free guide requests

When you request our free guide, we collect your email address, selected language, the page path and request time. We use Omnisend to store and process the request and send the guide you asked for. Requesting a guide does not subscribe you to other marketing emails or change an existing unsubscribe preference. We keep the request only as needed to fulfil it, handle delivery issues and maintain necessary operational records. Contact info@emailflow.uk to request deletion. See Omnisend’s privacy notice.

Website measurement and cookies

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand visits and page use. The integration removes query strings and fragments from page URLs before sending analytics events. This site does not send the enquiry fields as analytics event properties. Vercel describes its Web Analytics service as cookie-free; this does not describe the separate Calendly service. Calendly provides its own cookie choices when its booking service loads. See Vercel’s analytics privacy information for the data used by that service.

International processing

Our service providers may process information outside Lithuania, the European Economic Area or the UK. Calendly states that it hosts invitee data in the United States. Provider processing locations and transfer terms depend on the service and configuration. For details, see Calendly’s storage and transfer information and Supabase’s residency and transfer information. Contact us to request information about the arrangements applying to your enquiry.

How long information is kept

Retention depends on whether we are still responding to your enquiry, discussing work, providing a service or need the record for a legal or accounting obligation. Enquiry records should be deleted when no longer needed for those purposes. This covers stored enquiries and agency notifications as well as correspondence. A request to erase information is considered against any applicable obligation to retain records; it does not require an indefinite retention period.

Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you can request access, correction or deletion, ask us to restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request portability where it applies. If processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent. Contact info@emailflow.uk with your request.

You may complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate or, where applicable, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or your local supervisory authority.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the website enquiry and booking journey. Client-store customer data processed while delivering email marketing services needs to be covered by the relevant client agreement and data-processing terms. This page does not replace those arrangements.

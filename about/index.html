About EmailFlow

EmailFlow is a London-based email marketing agency serving UK ecommerce stores. Our work focuses on Omnisend, lifecycle automation, campaign management and deliverability support.

Who We Are

Erik Jevseicikas is the CEO of EmailFlow. Our focus is Omnisend email marketing for UK ecommerce stores: signup forms, customer journeys, regular campaigns and reporting. Meet Erik or contact info@emailflow.uk to discuss your store.

Based in London, United Kingdom, EmailFlow focuses on growing ecommerce stores with monthly revenue above £10,000. We focus entirely on customer retention, lifecycle automations, and email deliverability engineering.

Our Core Philosophy

We believe email marketing should not be an afterthought or a random monthly newsletter. When built correctly with automated flows, segmentation, and clean deliverability, email can support repeat purchases through relevant messages. We review performance against the store’s starting point, costs and customer behaviour.

What We Deliver

  • Flat-Fee Transparent Management: Full account management for £800/month with no hidden fees.
  • 7 Core Automated Flows: Welcome series, abandoned cart recovery, browse abandonment, post-purchase, and win-back flows, with scope and timing agreed after the audit.
  • Deliverability Protection: SPF, DKIM, DMARC engineering to support authentication and monitor inbox placement.
  • Platform Mastery: Our managed email services use Omnisend for Shopify stores.

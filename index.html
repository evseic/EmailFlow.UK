    How email marketing works

    Email earns from what you already have.

    Instead of paying for new traffic over and over, email helps you earn more from the people who have already visited your store.

    List growth

    Forms, pop-ups and purchase integrations. Eligible visitors can choose to subscribe; marketing permissions determine who can receive emails.

    Automated flows

    Welcome series, abandoned cart emails, post-purchase flows — everything sends itself based on customer behavior. The system works 24/7.

    Regular campaigns

    New products, promotions, educational content, seasonal pushes — timely reminders for people who already know and trust you.

    Dynamic segmentation

    Segmentation by behavior, purchase history and engagement — every customer gets emails about what actually matters to them.

    Data & A/B testing

    We see who opens, clicks and buys. We use that to optimize subject lines, copy and buttons — to learn which changes improve engagement and purchasing behaviour.

    Deliverability

    DKIM, SPF, DMARC and sender reputation management — we monitor authentication, sending practices and placement signals.

    Frequently asked questions

    Answers before you even ask.

    How quickly can email automation launch?

    Core flows can be planned for a seven-day implementation once account access, integrations, content and approvals are ready. Scope is agreed after the audit. Launch timing is separate from commercial results; revenue and list growth are not guaranteed.

    Which platforms do you work with?

    We work exclusively with Omnisend, including Shopify store integrations. We help with flows, campaigns, segmentation and deliverability.

    Which stores are a good fit?

    Our core service is designed for ecommerce stores with monthly revenue of £10,000 or more. Suitability also depends on list size, consent, purchase frequency and the work required. We assess these during the consultation.

    Do I need an existing email list?

    An established list helps, but signup forms and customer integrations can be part of the plan. List growth depends on store traffic, the offer and permission to send marketing; there is no fixed growth multiple.

    How much does email management cost?

    Management is £800 per month, excluding VAT, including the Omnisend subscription, with no setup fee, four campaigns per month and a minimum term of three months. Initial flow scope and the remaining commercial terms must be confirmed in writing before work starts. See the pricing guide for details.

    Can you guarantee inbox placement?

    No provider can guarantee placement in every inbox or the primary tab. Authentication, list hygiene, sending practices and monitoring help manage deliverability, but receiving mail services decide where messages appear.

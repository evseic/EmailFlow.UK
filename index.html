    How email marketing works

    Email earns from what you already have.

    Instead of paying for new traffic over and over, email helps you earn more from the people who have already visited your store.

    List growth

    Forms, pop-ups and purchase integrations. Everyone who shows interest lands in your database instead of disappearing after a single visit.

    Automated flows

    Welcome series, abandoned cart emails, post-purchase flows — everything sends itself based on customer behavior. The system works 24/7.

    Regular campaigns

    New products, promotions, educational content, seasonal pushes — timely reminders for people who already know and trust you.

    Dynamic segmentation

    Segmentation by behavior, purchase history and engagement — every customer gets emails about what actually matters to them.

    Data & A/B testing

    We see who opens, clicks and buys. We use that to optimize subject lines, copy and buttons — so every email drives more sales.

    Deliverability

    DKIM, SPF, DMARC and sender reputation management — your emails land in the inbox, not the spam folder.

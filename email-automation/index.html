Plan each flow before writing emails

An automation needs more than a template. Define the entry event, eligible audience, waiting period, conditions, exit rules and measurement method. Those decisions help avoid irrelevant messages and overlapping journeys.

A practical flow specification

  • Trigger: the subscriber, browsing, cart, checkout or purchase event.
  • Eligibility: consent, suppression, recent activity and previous flow participation.
  • Timing: delays that reflect the offer and buying cycle.
  • Exit: purchase, unsubscribe or another event that makes further messages inappropriate.
  • QA: test profiles, links, personalisation fallbacks and rendering before activation.

Which flows come first?

Prioritise based on missing journeys and meaningful event volume. A welcome flow and checkout recovery may be useful starting points; browse or site abandonment depends on identifying eligible contacts. Seven named flows are not seven mandatory flows for every store.

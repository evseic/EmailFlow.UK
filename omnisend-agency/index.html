From Omnisend setup to regular campaigns

We plan Omnisend around the store's customer journey: how people subscribe, which events trigger messages and how purchasers leave a recovery flow. Email and any SMS work must follow the permissions and agreed scope for that channel.

  1. Review the store integration, customer events, list sources and sender configuration.
  2. Agree the signup-form offer, template style and lifecycle priorities.
  3. Build and test welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back workflows.
  4. Review the campaign calendar, segmentation and attribution settings with the store.

What should be checked before migrating?

Inventory active workflows, suppression lists, consent records, templates and historical reports. Plan the cutover so the old and new accounts do not send overlapping journeys. Migration needs its own agreed scope; do not assume every historical metric transfers between platforms.

What an Omnisend account review should establish

Review account configuration, contact eligibility and message performance separately. A well-designed email cannot fix missing store events, and a connected store does not mean every contact should enter every workflow.

  • Sender setup: review authentication status and sending identity against the actual DNS configuration. Required changes depend on domain access and your existing mail setup.
  • Contact data: review list sources, subscription status, suppression and segment fields. Importing a contact does not establish marketing permission.
  • Workflows: inventory active triggers, filters, delays and exits before building replacements.
  • Reporting: record the period and attribution settings so later comparisons use consistent definitions.

Moving a programme to Omnisend

Inventory live messages and the data each needs. Agree migration scope, preserve suppression and consent records, test new journeys, then plan when old journeys stop. Keep historical reports separately where equivalent transfer is unavailable.

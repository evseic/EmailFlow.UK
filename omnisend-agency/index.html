From Omnisend setup to regular campaigns

We plan Omnisend around the store's customer journey: how people subscribe, which events trigger messages and how purchasers leave a recovery flow. Email and any SMS work must follow the permissions and agreed scope for that channel.

  1. Review the store integration, customer events, list sources and sender configuration.
  2. Agree the signup-form offer, template style and lifecycle priorities.
  3. Build and test welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back workflows.
  4. Review the campaign calendar, segmentation and attribution settings with the store.

Signup forms and welcome-flow checks

We review who sees each signup offer, how often it reappears and whether the promised code reaches the welcome email. Read our email popup timing and Omnisend setup guide for a practical checklist.

What should be checked before migrating?

Inventory active workflows, suppression lists, consent records, templates and historical reports. Plan the cutover so the old and new accounts do not send overlapping journeys. Migration needs its own agreed scope; do not assume every historical metric transfers between platforms.
